STRAIGHT ANSWERS

The things people want to ask before they call, and usually do not.

What does it cost?

There is no price list, because no two properties are the same size. What I can promise is that the number is worked out in front of you rather than guessed, and you get it in writing before anything starts. A one-off clean-up is usually a day. Ongoing maintenance is a set price per visit, agreed before the first one.

Is my job too small?

No. A single overgrown bed is a fine place to start, and plenty of my regular properties began as one job someone had been putting off. If it genuinely is not worth paying me for, I will tell you that too.

What do you not do?

I do not plow parking lots, I do not climb trees, and I do not take on fencing or structural work. Walks, steps and entries in winter, yes, by hand. Pruning from the ground, yes. Anything above that is a job for someone with the right ticket and the right machine.

How soon can you look at it?

Usually inside a week, because everything I look after is within about fifteen minutes of my own driveway. I reply {{quoteReplyTime}}, and the quote follows after I have walked the property, not before.

MORE THAN MAINTENANCE

Mowing and clean-ups are the week to week. These are the bigger one-off jobs: beds rebuilt, stone laid, a property set up so it looks after itself for years.

Garden beds rebuilt in stone

Four days on one Kelowna property. Tired beds stripped back and rebuilt in decorative rock, all by hand, with no heavy equipment anywhere near the lawn.

  1. 01

    The gardens cleaned up

    Every bed cleared of weeds and spent plants, the keepers pruned and left where they were, and a fresh edge cut the full length where the beds meet the grass.

  2. 02

    Fabric laid and stapled

    Landscape fabric laid over the lot and cut in around each plant that stayed, then pinned flat so it stays put. About 400 staples went in.

  3. 03

    Stone hauled and placed

    Twelve yards of rock wheeled in and spread by hand. That is 168 barrow loads, every one of them placed where it needed to go.

Lawn repaired where the barrows ran, and the site left clean.
